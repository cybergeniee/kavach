Try Kavach in one minute

Kavach listens to a phone call and warns an elderly person, out loud and in their own language, the moment the call turns into a scam. Pick any test below.

Play a recorded call

Plays through your speakers and runs through the live system. Turn your volume up.

  • “Digital arrest” scamHinglish · fake police, drugs parcel, asks for OTP. Red alarm in about 10 seconds.
  • Fake bank KYC scamTamil · warning is spoken in Tamil.
  • Customs parcel scamEnglish · fake FedEx and CBI, asks for a fee.
  • Grandson callingHinglish · a normal family call. Stays green, no alarm.

Test it with your own voice

Choose Live call, press Start listening, allow the microphone, and read one of these aloud. Or put a real call on speaker.

  • Scam“Main Mumbai crime branch se bol raha hoon. Aapke Aadhaar se drugs ka parcel mila hai. Aap digital arrest mein hain, kisi ko mat batana. Abhi OTP batao.”
  • Not a scam“Main Thane police station se bol raha hoon. Passport verification ke liye kal documents leke thane aa jaiye.” Real police can call too: this stays green or amber, never red.
  • Not a scam“Hello Dadi, main Rohan. Kal milne aaunga. Aapne dawai li?”
  • Scam“Congratulations, you won 25 lakh in KBC. Send 12,000 processing fee on UPI to claim it.”

Press Stop and get report at the end for a spoken summary and next steps.

Open it on your phone

Scan to open Kavach on a phone. Use Chrome or Safari and allow the microphone. Put a call on speaker next to it, or play a recorded call.

Watch as family

While a call is running, tap Family code at the top and scan it with a second phone. The family sees the transcript, the meter and the alarm live.
